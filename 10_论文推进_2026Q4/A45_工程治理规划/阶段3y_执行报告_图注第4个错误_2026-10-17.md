## 3. ★ 发现第 4 个图注错误：一个与产物不符的声称

### 3.1 原文
fig5 图注写：

> The LADWP Morris/Sobol configurations return **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE**
> (6–7 of 2048 Saltelli points fail the gate and the engine propagates NaN silently
> into the variance) and are therefore omitted rather than imputed.

### 3.2 与实际产物对照

`ne4_sobol_bootstrap_summary.json` 里两个配置的真实状态：

| 配置 | status | n_valid / n_valid_raw | n_degenerate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ha\|IEUA` | **OK** | 117 / 128 | 11 |
| `ha\|LADWP` | **OK** | **128 / 128** | **0** |

**两个配置都是 `OK`，LADWP 一个点都没丢。**

### 3.3 三处独立证据表明该说法是错的
1. **产物**：LADWP `status = OK`, `n_degenerate = 0`
2. **脚本**：`make_fig5.py` 里 LADWP 是**正常使用**的（计算 `sum_s1_ladwp` 并画在图上）
3. **触发条件**：`NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` 只在"有效点 < 32"时设置，LADWP 有 128 个有效点

### 3.4 而且这段自相矛盾
同一段文字里，**前面说** `n_eff = 128 (LADWP)`，**后面说** LADWP 返回 NOT_IDENTIFIABLE
并"被省略"。**若被省略，怎么还会有 n_eff = 128？**

### 3.5 修正
改为与产物一致的表述：

> Both Morris/Sobol configurations are identified: the LADWP configuration retains all 128 base
> points, while the IEUA configuration loses 11 of 128 to degenerate evaluations, which are
> dropped rather than imputed.

### 1.2 补写的内容（新增约 190 词）

> **One composition is genuinely adverse, and it is the saltiest one.** The table is not a set
> of equivalent panels. Figshare RW — the only composition whose dissolved solids sit close to
> the admissible limit (TDS 1,378 against 2,200 mg/L) — is the **only** one in which harmful
> outcomes outnumber beneficial ones (105 vs 102), whereas the two fully characterised,
> lower-salinity real compositions favour the city decisively (LADWP 186 vs 8; IEUA 151 vs 56).
> The ordering follows the mechanism rather than departing from it: the identity of §2 splits
> the outcome into a displacement gain and a quality penalty, and a composition near the limit
> drives the penalty term up until it offsets the gain. Two consequences are worth stating
> plainly. First, the "reclaimed cooling is beneficial" reading and its opposite are **both
> correct within this grid** and are separated by composition — which is exactly why a single
> facility-level number cannot settle the question. Second, the most adverse composition is also
> the least completely characterised (silica absent), so its 105/102 split is the panel most
> sensitive to further sample data; we flag it rather than lean on it.

**为什么这样写**：
- 它把新事实**接到已有机制上**（恒等式的"挤占增益 vs 质量惩罚"），而不是孤立地报告；
- 它**主动声明该数据的脆弱性**（silica 缺失 ⇒ 最敏感），防止审稿人指出后被动；
- 它把"两种读法都对"提升为一条方法论结论——正是论文想说的"设施级单一数字无法定论"。

## 2. 任务 B（意外发现）：撤回一个**错误归因**

### 2.1 发现的错误表述
- 主稿 L768：`a high-salinity source reverses the committed-regime result`
- SI L352：标题 `**A high-salinity source reverses the sign.**`
- SI L282：`occurs only for the high-salinity composition under severe quality tiers`

### 2.2 实测检验（决定性）
在 **committed 分支**（池被用尽，234 个可靠情景）按组成统计符号：

| composition | n | 有益 (dS<0) | 有害 (dS>0) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LADWP（TDS 22） | 56 | 39 | 8 |
| **IEUA（TDS 443）** | **60** | **3** | **48** |
| **Figshare（TDS 1,378）** | **60** | **3** | **48** |
| AI-UWM 假设 | 58 | 41 | 8 |

**IEUA 与 Figshare 的符号分布完全相同（3 / 48）。**
⇒ **"高盐度使符号翻转"不成立**。真正的分界是**质量约束是否绑定**（IEUA 与 Figshare 都绑定；LADWP 与 AI-UWM 不绑定），而不是盐度本身。

### 2.3 处置
| 位置 | 改法 |
|---|---|
| 主稿 | 删除"high-salinity reverses"；改为附注：*"An earlier reading attributed the reversal to high salinity specifically; the repaired grid does not support that — within the committed branch both IEUA and Figshare RW give 48 harmful against 3 beneficial, so the split tracks the quality-limited tier and the pool level rather than salinity alone."* |
| SI L352 | 标题改为 `**The highest-salinity composition is the only net-adverse one.**`，并加两句告诫：(i) 本网格中两种读法都对、由组成分开；(ii) **不要**读成"高盐度普遍翻转符号"，因为这四组同时混淆了盐度与数据完整性，不是受控的盐度扫描 |
| SI L282 | 从"occurs only for the high-salinity composition"改为"occurs for the compositions whose constraint binds under severe quality tiers — **not** for salinity as such"，并给出 3/48 对照 |

> ⚠️ 这是本项目第 **8** 项被撤回的主张。它与既有的"Figshare BW 不是再生水"、
> "95% 已承诺不可识别"等属同类：**用单个实例的巧合去解释一个机制**。

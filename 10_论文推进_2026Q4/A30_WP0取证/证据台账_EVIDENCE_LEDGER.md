## B. Lead 独立复算命令（可复核 A5–A11）

```bash
cd "C:/Users/mmrgr/Desktop/论文/算力中心/10_论文推进_2026Q4/A13_可靠性机制"
PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 python -c "
import pandas as pd
d=pd.read_csv('supply_state_regimes.csv')

print('strict:',(ds<0).sum(),(ds>0).sum(),(ds==0).sum())
print('tol1e-9:',(ds<-1e-9).sum(),(ds>1e-9).sum())
print('zero_disp:',(rel.displaced_ml==0).sum())
"
```

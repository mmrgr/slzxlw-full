# Phase 0 Competitor Gap

The detailed literature comparison is maintained in `literature/competitor_gap_analysis.md`. The Phase 0 conclusion is:

- Alissa and Xiao quantify lifecycle or national AI water-energy-carbon footprints.
- Wang quantifies global data-centre/WWTP symbiosis and reports large potential benefits.
- Han resolves peak public-water capacity; Alston resolves hourly generator-side water relocation.
- Saleh identifies water compatibility, constituent enrichment, treatment demand, and blowdown as missing dimensions in demand-only assessment.
- WaterMet2 supplies the urban metabolism precedent; Skerker shows that climate and infrastructure decisions can create distributional affordability burdens.

AI-UWM's possible addition is a paired B0/B1/B2 city-source estimand with explicit shared-versus-dedicated reclaimed allocation, incumbent-user displacement, and dynamic cooling-water quality. It remains a conditional hypothesis until G2/G3 and formal Q×L experiments are complete. No “world first” claim is justified.
